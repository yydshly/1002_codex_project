# Carnegie Mansion model · CC0 1.0

The `CooperHewitt_print.stl` asset is the Smithsonian / Cooper Hewitt Carnegie Mansion exterior printing model. Cooper Hewitt explicitly releases the mansion model under Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

- Creator and institution: 3D Systems, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum.
- Scan produced and donated in June 2014.
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Official license statement: [Download the Mansion](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/open-source-at-cooper-hewitt/mansionmodel/).
- Official original file: [CooperHewitt_print.stl](https://3d-api.si.edu/content/document/3d_package:5010a19c-2f7f-4325-b43c-bebe6711b473/resources/CooperHewitt_print.stl).
- Download mirror with preserved provenance: [3D-Printed.org](https://www.3d-printed.org/models/smithsonian-carnegie-mansion-exterior-55cce488).

The downloaded STL is unchanged. ATELIER applies a display translation and scale in its viewer; it does not claim these unitless STL coordinates are full-size architectural measurements. The STL has no original material or photographic texture. The viewer supplies a presentation material for a monochrome architectural model.

Cooper Hewitt suggests attribution, contribution of improvements, responsible use, and accurate representation of the dataset and its source. ATELIER provides those credits and describes the actual exterior printing-model scope.
