The cat photographer directly published their own work under CC0. The original licensing statement and its page URL are recorded in `manifest.json`. This photograph does not rely on Unsplash's old or current license.

The Met's [Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access) releases public-domain artwork images and collection metadata under CC0. The three original API responses in `provenance/` each explicitly record `isPublicDomain: true` and the official image URL. An ancient artist's death date alone was not used to infer the availability of a museum photograph.
